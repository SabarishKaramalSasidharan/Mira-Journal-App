# Mira Research Archive

Archived research reports for **Mira**, the journaling app. Each report distills an interactive Cursor canvas into a readable, versioned Markdown document (verdicts, data tables, competitor comparisons, ratings, roadmaps, and citations).

## Reports

- **[Journaling apps in 2026 — where Mira fits](./journal-competitive-analysis.md)** — Market sizing, a full feature matrix against 7 rivals (Rosebud, Day One, Apple Journal, Daylio, Reflection, Mindsera, How We Feel), where each competitor wins/loses, a positioning strategy, and a retention playbook. Verdict: compete on **positioning and retention**, not features; Mira's only real gap is long-term memory, its edge is on-device privacy.
- **[Mira brand color — cut the picker, own the teal](./brand-color-strategy.md)** — Competitive color landscape, hue-crowding analysis, color psychology, and a rating of the 6 shipped palettes. Verdict: **cut the theme picker** and commit to "Mira Teal" (`#0e9e8c` light / `#2dd4bf` dark), including light/dark tokens and WCAG AA notes.
- **[What more can Mira's Reflect tab do?](./reflect-tab-ideas.md)** — 19 ideas across three tiers (Quick / Medium / Ambitious) on an effort-vs-impact map, with per-idea value/data/effort/risk tables and a recommended 5-item build order. Guardrail: deepen the calm "mirror," don't build a dashboard.
- **[Mira's completion moment — reflect back, don't applaud](./completion-moment.md)** — A critique of the "Saved!" modal, how 7 apps handle the completion moment, six design options, a before→after concept, and a celebrate-vs-quiet trigger rule. Verdict: default to a non-blocking **reflect-back close** and gate confetti to milestones.
- **[Mira — launch & monetization strategy](./launch-monetization-strategy.md)** — An honest readiness scorecard (14 dimensions), the leanest path to the App Store & Google Play (Capacitor wrap + Apple 4.2 workaround), a phased roadmap (harden → store MVP → monetize → grow), a freemium model (**Mira Plus $4.99/mo · $39.99/yr · $99.99 lifetime**), and a lean budget with a **~$124–136 minimum-to-launch** total. Verdict: it's a polished prototype, not a shippable product — fix durability, trust, and packaging, and gate depth not writing.
- **[How journaling apps make money — and Mira's path to profitability](./competitor-monetization.md)** — The *business* companion: how 11 competitors actually earn (exact 2026 price tiers, revenue/scale with confidence flags, and the company behind each), the **indie-studio / portfolio pattern** that dominates the category, the AI-journaling **LLM-cost-vs-price** insight (text ≈ cents, voice is the real cost driver), and a concrete profitability path for Mira — unit economics (~80% margin), RevenueCat 2026 conversion benchmarks, and break-even math (**~15–20 subscribers covers all fixed costs**). Verdict: copy the lean-freemium shape, but exploit Mira's text-first on-device AI to price ~60% under the AI leaders.
- **[Action plan — from here to first paying customers](./action-plan.md)** — The founder's do-this-next roadmap: a 🟢/🟡/🔴 "where we are today" snapshot of the current build, then **7 sequenced phases** (finalize UX → trust & measurable → package as apps with Capacitor → wire the paywall → control AI cost → soft launch → public launch to break-even), each with plain-language goal, concrete steps, "done when," time, and cost. Includes a money map, a **~$124–136 minimum-spend-to-launch** budget, a "first 2 weeks" quick-start checklist, and risks. Reuses the pricing/break-even numbers from the monetization docs (**break-even ≈ 15–20 subs**).
- **[Mira's mood selector — go Hybrid (1–5 + optional emotion tag)](./mood-selector-options.md)** — Nine mood-selector concepts (weather, Inside Out–inspired faces, Mira mascot, classic 5-face, color orbs, word chips, slider, grouped two-tier wheel, hybrid) judged on the decisive scale-vs-categorical axis, with pros/cons, a full comparison matrix, a collision-resolved color system, and duty-of-care guardrails. Verdict: ship the **Hybrid** — a one-tap ordered 1–5 opener that keeps the chart plus an optional named-emotion tag, with the weather scale kept live to A/B.
- **[Mira's emotion faces — ship the mascot-droplet](./emotion-glyph-styles.md)** — Four premium SVG face styles (Mira mascot-droplet, glossy 3D, soft flat, hand-drawn doodle) compared on brand fit, smiley feel, expressiveness, small-size legibility, and scale, plus a color-blind-safe accessibility proof. Verdict: ship the **mascot-droplet faces** — distinctly Mira, still a friendly smiley, one shape-driven system for both the 1–5 ladder and the emotion tags.
- **[Grouping Mira's emotion tags by color](./emotion-grouping-options.md)** — Coloring the 16 emotion droplets by **group** instead of 16 hues: Option A (3 groups — Pleasant / Difficult / Low·Numb) vs Option B (2 groups — Pleasant / Unpleasant), with group colors, WCAG AA contrast, and colorblind-safety notes. Direction chosen: **Option B**, the quietest two-color split.
- **[Where Mira's optional "feeling" tag should live](./emotion-entrypoint-options.md)** — Three entry points for the optional emotion tag to replace the orphaned floating pill: **A** a composer emoji button, **B** an in-chat invite from Mira, **C** folded into the mood step — with pros/cons and best-fit notes. **B and C were built behind a Settings toggle** for testing, with A as the on-demand fallback.

## `canvases/`

The [`canvases/`](./canvases) folder holds the **original Cursor canvas sources** (`*.canvas.tsx`) that these reports were generated from, preserved verbatim. They import from `cursor/canvas` and are only meant to run inside Cursor's canvas host — they are intentionally excluded from the app's TypeScript build and lint (see the repo's `tsconfig.app.json` include and `.oxlintrc.json` ignore patterns).
