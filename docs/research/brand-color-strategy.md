## The recommendation, up front

Ship **one** color and hide the picker. Commit to the current default teal, **"Mira Teal" #0e9e8c** (light) / **#2dd4bf** (dark). The name (mirror / reflection), the teal reflection-droplet mascot, and teal's psychology (calm _and_ growth) all point to the same hue — and unlike blue, orange, or purple, no journaling headliner owns teal yet. A rotating accent would recolor the mascot and erase the one thing a young brand needs most: a color people remember.

## Color psychology for a reflection app

Fit = suitability for calm, empathetic, anti-blank-page journaling.

| Hue | Hex | Connotes | Fit for Mira |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Teal** | `#0e9e8c` | Calm + growth; "alert stillness"; trust without coldness; renewal | ✅ Best — soothes without sedating; on-name (mirror / still water) |
| Blue | `#4276ce` | Trust, calm, clarity — but sedating & corporate | 🟡 Partial — safe but generic and the most crowded lane |
| Green | `#2f9e44` | Growth, nature, balance | 🟡 Partial — skews productivity / eco / finance, less "reflective" |
| Indigo | `#6366f1` | Introspection, creativity, "night" | 🟡 Partial — evocative but higher-arousal and already taken |
| Amber | `#e08a00` | Warmth, optimism, energy | ❌ Poor — high-arousal; Headspace territory; weakest AA on white |
| Rose | `#e23a6d` | Care, warmth, passion | ❌ Poor — romance / high-arousal, wrong register for reflection |

**Why teal specifically:** Teal is the rare hue that borrows blue's trust and lowered arousal while keeping green's growth and renewal — research calls it "alert stillness": it soothes without sedating. WGSN even named "Transformative Teal" its Colour of the Year 2026. For an app about looking inward and coming out a little clearer, that duality is the brief.

## The pick: "Mira Teal" in light & dark

Keep the exact tokens already tuned in **palette.ts** — they're well chosen. The move is to **promote the default to the brand color**, name it, and build the identity system around it.

### Light theme tokens

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| accent | `#0e9e8c` |
| strong | `#0b8072` |
| accent text (body copy) | `#0a6d61` |
| soft | `#d7f3ee` |

### Dark theme tokens

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| accent | `#2dd4bf` |
| strong | `#14b8a6` |
| soft | `#0f302c` |
| surface | `#04241f` |

**Accessibility & dark-mode behavior:** White on `#0e9e8c` lands near **3.3:1** — passes WCAG AA for large text and UI components (buttons, the mascot), which is exactly why palette.ts pairs it with a darkened **#0a6d61** for accent-colored body copy (AA ≥ 4.5). In dark mode the brighter **#2dd4bf** clears AA comfortably on the near-black surface. Teal is genuinely easier to hit AA in both themes than amber (Honey), whose light value struggles on white.

## The picker: keep or cut?

### Cut it now — _(Recommended)_

- **Brand recognition compounds.** A young brand's scarcest asset is a color people can recall. Six rotating accents = no color memory.
- **The mascot IS the accent.** Its body is the `--accent → --accent-strong` gradient. A purple Mira isn't Mira — theming the accent breaks the character.
- **The strong brands commit.** Calm (blue), Headspace (orange), Day One (blue) own one color. The picker-first apps — Reflectly, Daylio, Diarly — are exactly the ones with no identity color.

### The honest counter-arguments (and the middle path)

- **Personalization delights & retains** — the "magic color change" is a real dopamine moment (Reflectly leans on it).
- **Accessibility** — some users need to shift hue for color sensitivity or contrast.

So don't delete the code — **hide it**. Ship one teal now, then reintroduce theming later as a deliberate, possibly-gated "delight" that recolors UI accents _but never the mascot_, plus a dedicated high-contrast / colorblind-friendly mode that earns its keep on accessibility rather than vanity.

## How this makes Mira stand out

Mira owns a specific **"mirror-water" teal** — deeper and greener than Day One's airy sky blue, brighter than Calm's navy, and nowhere near Headspace's orange or Reflectly's violet. Yes, teal appears in wellness (Insight Timer, Finch) — but never on a journaling headliner, and never _embodied by a character_. One committed hue + one teal reflection-droplet that literally is that hue = a brand you recognize from the home-screen icon alone. That compounding recognition is worth far more to a young app than a color picker ever could be.
